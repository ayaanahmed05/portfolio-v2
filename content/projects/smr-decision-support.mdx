### Overview
Designed and prototyped a safety-critical AI decision support system for Small Modular Reactor (SMR) control room operations. Developed in part for the CI Competition 2026 and grounded in 2 research papers, the platform integrates a 6-subsystem architecture pairing real-time digital twin analytics with dynamic multi-agent AI to monitor plant state, evaluate operator risk, and deliver explainable, step-by-step guidance during critical interventions.

### Key Features & Engineering

* **6-Subsystem Architecture:** Designed the complete system blueprint based on 2 foundational research papers—defining application components, API interfaces, data pipelines, and safety requirements across all operational modules.
* **Competition-Grade Design:** Built as part of the CI Competition 2026, tailoring the application architecture to meet rigorous safety, human factors, and operational benchmarks.
* **Dual-Agent Safety Workflow:** Engineered a multi-agent AI system featuring a dedicated Safety Supervisor agent that validates proposed operator actions against safety protocols before execution.
* **Explainable AI (XAI) Guidance:** Built conversational reasoning models that translate complex plant telemetry and human-factors data into clear, step-by-step diagnostic insights for operators.
* **Scenario-Based Validation:** Defined software testing measures to benchmark recovery times and prevent unsafe actions, driving rapid iterative optimization across simulated emergency scenarios.
